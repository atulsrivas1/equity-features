# EF3 robust history, volume pace and mean-ratio implementation

[EQ-155 #489](https://github.com/atulsrivas1/equity-features/issues/489), [EQ-156 #490](https://github.com/atulsrivas1/equity-features/issues/490) and [EQ-241 #666](https://github.com/atulsrivas1/equity-features/issues/666) library layer: the pure `ef3.robust_history` (`robust.median`, `robust.mad`, `robust.quantile`, `robust.percentile_rank`, `cohort.percentile_rank`, `cohort.rank`), `ef3.volume_pace_robust` (`pace.baseline_median`, `pace.baseline_mad`, `pace.ratio`, `pace.robust_z`) and `ef3.volume_ratio_mean` (`mean.numerator`, `mean.baseline`, `mean.ratio`) producers in `packages/features` (module `equity_features.ef3`, next to the daily-grid producers). The frozen [contract](ROBUST_PACE_CONTRACT_PROPOSAL.md) (versions `ef3-robust1`, `ef3-pace1`, `ef3-mean1`, decisions DP-*, rulings OQ-* and its coordinator freeze) governs; this slice implements it and redefines nothing. The EF3 I/O mapping (#611), worker (#612) and release acceptance (EQ-157) layers remain separate.

## Structure

`packages/features/src/equity_features/ef3/`: `_stats` (exact-rational median, MAD, type 7 quantile, mid-rank, one rounding), `_robust_common` (config envelope, closed decode, quality vocabulary with the contract precedence, `sessions` role admission, daily series admission, evidence helpers), `_robust_roles` (population, cohort values, bucket grid and bars admission), `_robust_history`, `_pace`, `_mean` (config, readiness, evidence, compute), `_robust_definitions` (own discovery) and `_robust_dispatch` (`compute_ef3_robust`, routed by config identity). The existing `_common`, `_dispatch`, `_definitions` and the daily-grid producers are unchanged and `__init__` only gains an appended import and `__all__` block, so the daily-grid `compute_ef3` signature (which needs a `DailyGrid`) is untouched: these producers take the governed grid from the `sessions` role, exactly as the contract specifies. The R17 catalog, `describe_intraday()` and its digest, the shared validators, the default registry and every EF1/EF2/EF3-D output are unchanged; no package version is bumped.

## Public surface

For each producer: `build_*_config` (explicit keyword parameters, no default except `evidence_limit`; the target `SessionSpec`, availability, adjustment and a mandatory `PriceUnit`), `decode_*_config` (closed envelope, byte-identical rebuild) and a `compute_*` function taking the fixed nullable role slots `(series, sessions, population, cohort_values)`, `(grid, sessions, bars)` or `(series, sessions)`, the target `EntityKey` and the selected `features`. A selection fixes the optional parameters: `quantile_num`/`quantile_den` are present exactly when `robust.quantile` is selected, `min_population` exactly when a cohort output is selected, `scale_num`/`scale_den` exactly when `pace.robust_z` is selected; any other combination is `INVALID_CONFIG`. `ef3_robust_definitions(config)` and `require_ef3_robust_capability` provide discovery and the batch-only capability check.

## Mathematics and numeric policy

All comparisons, sorts, medians, deviations, ranks and sums run on exact integers and `Fraction`s; a price coefficient is divided by `10^scale` only when `robust.median`, `robust.mad` or `robust.quantile` is published, and each published float64 is one correctly rounded `float(Fraction)` (exact zero is `+0.0`). `cohort.rank` is an exact int64 and is never converted through float. Cumulative pace operands and window sums are Python integers, so no `OVERFLOW` path exists for them (fixtures P24, R13). The quantile uses the exact reduced rational `p`, so `p = 1/3` over four values gives `h = 1` exactly (H24b).

## Role admission, readiness, counts and evidence

The shared `validate_batch` runs first (so a duplicate or unordered row is `DUPLICATE` or `INVALID_ORDER` before any identity check), then the producer checks namespace, unit (`INVALID_UNIT`), adjustment (`UNSUPPORTED_ADJUSTMENT`), declared order, target instrument, exact session bounds, the `x1:B(id)` reference grammar, grid and bucket identity and the exclusion facts, all with existing codes (RF01 to RF48). `require_compatible_inputs` runs between `series` and `cohort_values` only. Per-slot causes follow the contract precedence; every applicable reason is reported in lexical order and the status follows the first. Counts, reasons and the closed rules (`insufficient_history` when fewer than N governed sessions precede the target or `expected < m`; an exclusion fact with null or later knowledge blocks and does not count as an exclusion; early-close prior sessions are removed from expected; cohort `partial_universe` ignores the target member, which reports its own reasons) are as in the contract. Evidence rows are emitted for available outputs only, in the contract order, with the ids `session:<id>`, `target:<id>` (pace), `member:<id>`, `excluded:<id>` and `ineligible:<id>`.

## Notes, gaps and contract nits (recorded, nothing redefined)

1. **Evidence limit is result-wide.** The shared `FeatureResult` validator bounds the total evidence rows by `evidence_limit`, while the contract fixtures list the witnesses per output (H29a, H29b, C19, P29, R17). A single-output selection reproduces each fixture list; a joint selection truncates in lexical output order.
2. **Excluded and ineligible witnesses.** The contract lists them with boundary `completed_interval`, but the shared validator admits that boundary only for BAR and DAILY inputs. They are emitted as `use="excluded"`, `exclusion_reason=ineligible`, boundary `ordinary`, bound to the `sessions` REFERENCE input, with the session bounds as effective interval and the exclusion (or bounds) fact knowledge. Their row ids and order are as in the contract.
3. **Prefix fixtures with later sessions (H25, R16, P18).** Rows of a session after the target end after the market cutoff and the cutoff rule is `BOUNDS` (RF06), so they are constructible only when the cutoff is at or after them. H25 and R16 do not depend on the cutoff and match exactly. P18 as written cannot be built: moving the cutoff behind the appended session changes the evaluation bucket (`min(close offset, cutoff offset)`), so the literal construction is refused with `BOUNDS`. P18 was verified instead with the target closing at offset 200 and the cutoff behind the appended session, where the evaluation bucket is still 1 and the result equals P01b. This is a fixture construction nit of the contract, not a library gap.
4. **Coverage naming.** The contract fixes no coverage entry names. Optional per-slot `IntervalCoverage` entries are `session:<session_id>` (series), `member:<instrument_id>` (cohort values) and `bucket:<session_id>:<index>` (bars), with the exact slot bounds; a delivered row without an entry is read as complete, an entry with no row is `governed_gap`, and no row without an entry is `absent_input`.
5. **Absent roles.** An absent `sessions` or `grid` slot makes every pace and history output `absent_input` with observed 0 (expected N, rank N+1, ratio and z 2); an absent `population` makes the cohort outputs `absent_input` with `expected` `None` (the declared size is unknown). A `series` or `bars` batch supplied without the `sessions` role is not examined or bound.
6. **Knowledge of grid facts.** The `known_at` of `ef3.session.bounds`, `ef3.bucket.edge` and `ef3.population.member` facts is carried into evidence but is not assessed; only exclusion facts and data rows are knowledge-gated, as the contract states.
7. **Pace precedence.** If no grid bucket fits the actual target session, `ineligible` takes precedence over `future_market` even when the cutoff is also early (the contract pins P10 and P25 separately only).
8. **Units.** `robust.median`, `robust.mad` and `robust.quantile` columns carry the operand unit (`shares` or `currency/share`) per the contract `unit_by_field`; the `FeatureColumn.unit` is the resolved unit, not the ledger string `operand-defined`.
9. **Whole-tree boundary check.** `tools/check_boundary.py` fails on the unchanged base commit at `ef1/_reference_close.py` (`import base64`); the `violations` policy applied to every file of `equity_features.ef3`, including the new modules, reports nothing.
10. The header-ledger amendment and the shared `CONTRACT.json` are not amended here.

## Author source validation

A standalone manual script (not committed, no test files added) expanded the compact fixture notation into canonical DAILY, BAR and REFERENCE batches and compared every output (exact bits, `+0.0` bits, the R17 tolerance `max(8 ulp, 1e-12 |r|)` or exact int64, as each fixture states), status, reasons, expected and observed counts and witness ids with the frozen contract. The author reports 44/44 history, 24/24 cohort, 37/37 pace and 19/19 mean-ratio result fixtures matched (P18 through the adjusted construction in note 3) and 48/48 refusal fixtures matched (80 variants; the author-reported figures are not independently confirmed here). Known-at and reconstruction availability give identical values, quality and evidence. A before/after snapshot of the R17 result, the eight-entry R17 describe and digest, the EF2 money-flow, momentum, channels and realized-variance catalog digests, the EF2 retracement result and discovery and the EF3-D relative, log volatility and realized variance results and discovery was byte-identical. The author reports mypy strict clean on the new and touched `ef3` modules (author-reported).

## Cost

Author-observed on one machine, not a performance claim: a 4096-session history window computes median, MAD, quantile and rank in about 0.23 s (exact rationals, `O(n log n)`; batch only, no incremental state).
