## Design invariants and open decisions

Keep the two source-independent distributions and public owned CanonicalBatch/ConfigSpec/FeatureResult contracts. Caller supplies ordered governed session grid, exact UTCns, units/basis, target, initialization anchor, C/K/E/revision/reference identities and coverage. No fetching calendars, references, benchmarks or missing dependencies. No sorting/deduplicating, holiday inference, gap compression, zero filling or automatic reset of recursive state. Finite windows recover only when their exact required slots recover; recursive anchor-to-target gaps require explicit new epoch or replay. Absent fields affect only dependent features.

Freeze concrete callable signatures, historical context/state and structured bucket/universe outputs in owning plans before code. Contract evolution must be versioned and qualified, not a parallel untyped dictionary API. Source acquisition/persistence remains outside core. Independent exact rational/high-precision references establish arithmetic; qualified Float64 uses stated relative/absolute1e-12 tolerance. Check wide price sums/products and stable centered variance; no wrap, unstable raw-moment subtraction or invented annualization. Qualify long flat RSI continuations against underflow without relabeling nonneutral initialized RSI as50. Avoid unbounded recursive Fraction denominators in retained streaming state; choose and test a bounded numerical representation explicitly.

Advertise only modes actually qualified for each ID. Batch implementations first; supported update/restore/state-replay modes must have bounded sufficient context, source/config/anchor/basis identities, gap/correction semantics and meaningful parity. No arbitrary EMA/RSI/ATR state merge or averaging partition indicators. Do not automatically expose history through the session accumulator. General custom-feature registration remains R3.
