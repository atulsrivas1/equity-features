## Mathematics, timing and authority

The caller supplies finite observations, exact price scales, same-currency
asset-conversion declarations, delivery coverage certificates and timestamped
feed-state intervals. The library does not discover calendars, holidays, DST,
market hours, price sources, FX or asset equivalence. Structural validity and
internal consistency are not proof those declarations describe a real venue.

Basis uses latest already-known eligible backward observations. Freeze compares
every supplied population row with the held pre-pause reference; its mean is
count weighted. Reopening fixes the boundary observations before inspecting
knowledge: an unknown or late first reference cannot be replaced by a later row.
Mapping follows the selected continuous event, including when an unused mapping
changes at reopening. Reduced rational stages are exact and checked; overflow
refuses rather than clipping, reordering or switching to float. Full input
identities bind unused supplied facts as well as consumed proof pointers.

Seven readiness roles preserve independent causes, selected identities, proofs
and original availability. Scheduled freeze differs from an outage or stale,
unknown, absent or not-yet-known delivery. Reproducing an unavailable result does
not make its metrics available. The example's Friday-through-Thursday names are
synthetic caller labels on a supplied UTC-nanosecond epoch, not an exchange
calendar or factual market-hours claim. No launch-status, liquidity, token
equivalence, economic price-discovery, trading or provider-rights claim follows.
