# R8 verification and acceptance strategy

Prepared October14,2026 with [R8 handoff](R8_AUTONOMOUS_HANDOFF.md). Proposed protocol only; no new tests, benchmark, review, artifact or acceptance was executed for this packet. Each story freezes its exact applicable protocol before Ready; concrete formulas and supported package/platform choices belong to that story's reviewed plan.

## Performance and conditional acceleration

- Bind baseline and candidate source, interpreter/dependency/backend versions, configuration/fixture/input/output identities and actual installed origins. Reuse accepted synthetic workload definitions and independent goldens; do not relabel old samples as a new candidate comparison.
- Separate unprofiled end-to-end elapsed samples from diagnostic profiles and isolated conversion/codec calls. Retain all raw runs, repeat count, median/range and explicit warm/cold/cache/GC/thread/host boundaries; whole-pipeline latency and standalone kernel latency answer different questions. Nonadditive cumulative frames, opaque native calls and whole-process high-water remain honestly labeled.
- Freeze workload/parameters/sample protocol and declared success criteria before runs. Full result/status/quality/evidence/identity parity is mandatory. Preserve negative, timeout, failed and inconclusive results. More workers, parallel defaults or a native backend require measured benefit on the admitted target with memory/thread/capacity limits and no correctness loss; shared-host noise is not a universal speedup.
- Targeted codec/materialization changes must preserve canonical bytes, precision/int64/ns/Decimal/null/timing identities, valid Unicode, surrogate rejection, mutation/ownership isolation, invalid-schema/type/identity denial and full producer/sink/receipt consistency. No weakened revalidation, hidden cache, reordered publication or broadened concurrency under a performance label.
- EQ086/EQ087 may deliver reviewed no-change/no-new-accelerator decisions. Explicitly show why accepted attribution does not justify a selected native kernel; retain existing supported fallback/packaging facts and no-impact decision. Applicable docs/source checks suffice when executable inputs are unchanged; do not invent native build proof.

## Strategy/scanner contracts and independent fixtures

- Freeze actual supported formula families, units, config/rule/version/input identities, feature availability and universe/coverage policies. Separate pure deterministic feature-consumer logic from acquisition, orchestration and publication.
- Hand-derived small synthetic fixtures establish expected rankings and selections independently of implementation. Include ties, stable secondary keys, input permutations, duplicate identities, empty/single/all-missing populations, partial coverage, invalid/nonfinite values, threshold equality, top-k edges, unavailable inputs and cutoff transitions. Missing is never silently zero or a tradable signal.
- Verify accepted core results are consumed rather than recomputed. Determinism includes complete output ordering, readiness/quality/evidence/identity and same-config repeat parity. Alternative execution modes must return complete identical results. Reject incompatible schema/config/source revisions.

## Forward labels and causality

- Definitions state horizon/calendar/endpoint interpolation policy, supplied price/corporate-action basis, formula/units/zero/negative handling, eligibility, availability/known-at and revisions. Independently derive tiny price paths, dividends/splits and missing-endpoint examples; do not copy implementation helpers to generate expected outputs.
- Exercise pending -> mature transitions, exact boundary times, incomplete horizons, revision invalidation, conflicting identities and adjusted/unadjusted basis mismatch. Future fields live only in label outputs and cannot enter signal feature schemas, ranking inputs or as-of selection. The same observed prefix must give identical signal outputs when future paths vary.
- Backtest examples demonstrate causal consumption with explicit maturity filters and supplied synthetic data. They qualify integration, not profitability, execution fill realism or provider rights.

## Distribution and final delivery gates

For actual new/changed packages, verify optional installation/import independence, supported fallback/thread policies, version/schema compatibility, meaningful numerical/causal tests, typing/boundary checks and public synthetic examples. Fresh supported Windows/Linux wheel and sdist installations use actual published artifacts and reject source-tree fallback. Audit every artifact's current head/size/digest/expiry and complete entries, RECORDs, source/sdist materialization and supported native dependency maps, as applicable to the changed distributions. Do not imply all-host/whole-kernel/ABI closure from tested archive combinations.

Review final source/contracts/docs independently with identity, exact head and findings disposition. Keep self-checks, CI, independent review, installed qualification and release acceptance distinct. After default-branch publication verify the actual relevant main checks and artifacts/source equivalence before Released; record acceptance then Done/closed story. Do not skip required gates, but do not rebuild unrelated packages or create endless metadata-only acceptance receipt cycles when unchanged reviewed evidence is sufficient under the applicable policy.
