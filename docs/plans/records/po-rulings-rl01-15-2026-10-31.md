PR1 also reads and prints the D2 marker and banner (generation side). PR2 adds the marker expiry gate (fail if the marker is present and the catalogue has the DV1 family), R5-R8, and one negative fixture per rule, including one each for X6 (stale pointer, unresolved pointer).

7. Wording tests (criterion 7) ship in PR1: no provider names, no banned words ("covered", "fixed", "production", "releasable", performance claims). The word "registry" is allowed.
