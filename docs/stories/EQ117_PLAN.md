## Proposed decision rules for owner acceptance

1. Correctness: all 320 fixed case/arm/OS/repetition observations exactly correct, zero clean false positives, all 240 eligible replay entries match, and zero calculator calls for all 80 missing/corrupt observations. Every failure or unrun slot remains in the denominator; no inferred PASS.
2. Overhead: for every OS, case and process state, evidence storage is at most twice baseline closed-file bytes plus 32 KiB per case; each measured-stage median is at most twice baseline plus 5 ms. Report all raw values, differences and spread, including failures. These are proposed engineering budgets, not measured facts or universal standards.
3. Optional automation signal: at least 20% fewer traced semantic operations by the ratio of arm medians across all five scheduled repeats on at least six of eight cases in each OS/process state, with no correctness loss. This is a task-tooling result, not a human debugging claim. Report contrary cases. All operation categories/internal calls must be disclosed; a wrapper is not one step when it hides multiple operations.
4. Product/witness: automated results alone cannot produce product GO or justify an independent witness. With no authorized human evidence, publish DEFER and an explicit owner go/defer/no-go decision request after the study. EQ118/119 remain conditional Backlog unless their actual requirements and explicit selection are met. Their open disposition cannot silently close E17/milestone14.

## Initial planning review corrections

Separate review identified four preparation gaps before any publication or evaluation: solver visibility could leak producer verdicts, the operation-reduction aggregation was unspecified, normative raw fields omitted timing endpoints/return code, and reversing case order cancelled the positional arm-order parity rule. The corrected proposal freezes visible files/fields and forbidden answer sources, distinguishes declared versus recomputed diagnostics (missing-input recomputation UNKNOWN), chooses ratio of arm medians with all scheduled slots required, lists same-clock endpoints and return codes explicitly, and counterbalances arm order using stable case indices (5/5 overall,2/3 per process state reversed in the other). Original REWORK evidence is retained outside the repository; no failed evaluation or runtime failure is implied.
