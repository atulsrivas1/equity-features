# C1 - Community onboarding and participation

Owner requested stories and a release after existing releases on October 7, 2026.
This is a coordinated documentation/community release: R14 in equity-features
and M5 in strategy-research. Milestones and plans do not constitute publication.
Live Projects own lifecycle status; all seven new stories start Backlog.

## Goal and audience

Give Python/quant developers a useful credential-free example, a concrete first
contribution and predictable support. Give research readers a clear route through
sanitized evidence and useful non-code participation. Equity-features is the
main entry point; the core, I/O, workers, research and account/profile owners
retain their responsibilities. No new library algorithm or strategy experiment.

## Measurement and decision

Freeze metric definitions before any authorized pilot: unique example attempts,
useful feedback reports, first contributions, returning participants and response
times. Record the initial baseline and evidence source; inaccessible counts are
unknown. Distinguish maintainer command replay from actual participant usability.
Proposed learning goals: five example users, three useful feedback reports and
one returning contributor. These are not promised outcomes or release thresholds.

One optional 30-day manual pilot follows authorization. Record observed feedback,
zero responses, failures and limitations, then continue/change/stop. Without
authorization, deliver a prepared/deferred pilot and an explicit reason; no
fabricated adoption, commercial demand or unsolicited contact. Do not publish
private participant details. No automatic reminder or schedule.

## Acceptance and delivery

- Every assigned story has its actual scope, documentation, applicable checks,
  unresolved limitations and delivery evidence reconciled.
- Quickstart and demonstrations run from the documented delivered channel in a
  clean environment; expected results are independently checked.
- Capability/version claims match accepted source/artifact receipts. Research
  verdicts, proxy limitations and private-replay boundaries are accurate.
- Starter issues are actually actionable and have acceptance/check instructions.
- Public changes receive completed separate Codex review covering final heads,
  findings disposition and applicable CI. A request/reaction/self-review is not
  completion. Account and companion changes retain their own review policies.
- Receipt binds exact delivered files/versions, reviews, checks, published readback,
  privacy/rights limits, participant observations or explicit pilot defer, and
  the next bounded decision. No empty tag to satisfy a milestone.
- Backlog -> Ready -> In progress -> Code review -> Test -> Ready to release ->
  Released -> Done. Close R14 and M5 only after their scoped delivery qualifies;
  C1 acceptance reconciles both. Growth goals can remain unmet.
