## Constraints that optimization cannot relax

- Admission of malformed supplied structures is a real common edge; missing high/low readiness remains specific to consuming outputs. Bands use scheduled grid slots, never compacted eligible rows. EMA/RSI/ATR recurrences retain exact anchor, seed, initialization and revision order.
- ATR target-close independence applies when target exceeds anchor. At anchor==target, warm-up observes the one anchor close only. Band width/position consume unrounded compatible witnesses, not rounded public bounds; preserve the large-offset cancellation fixtures and existing whole-call overflow contract.
- Compatible reuse requires complete immutable input, population, grid/window/anchor, configuration, cutoff, source/revision, algorithm and lifetime identity. Same symbol, object label or similar schema is insufficient. Generic callbacks are trusted caller code, not automatically pure or thread-safe.
- A combined task owns a complete FeatureResult and once-global evidence cap. Splitting calculation must preserve complete headers, quality, unavailable reasons, identity and deterministic joining. Keep actual universe joins and coordinator/sink commit ownership.
- CPU, native threads, parent/children, queues, retained inputs/results, IPC, I/O and publication share one job budget with explicit OS/other-job reserves. Distinguish declared, observed and physically enforced limits. Preserve rejection, cancellation, partial-commit and replay semantics; postcommit rejection does not imply rollback.
