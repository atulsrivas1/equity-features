## Civil-period mathematics and target inclusion

Let T be supplied target civil date; membership is start_date<=session.civil_date<end_date_exclusive. Week is ISO Monday through next Monday (including cross-year ISO weeks); month first through next month's first; year January1 through next January1. Current selection chooses the period containing T. Prior selection chooses the immediately preceding civil week/month/year, even if its market population is unavailable; never choose latest available prices. For rolling prior_complete, end=T. For rolling current_to_cutoff, end=T+one civil day. Rolling-weeks start=end-7*count civil days; rolling-months start=end shifted back count months, day clamped to destination month end. Preserve P03 February28/May31 and52-week examples. These are civil spans, not fixed observation counts.

Scheduled sessions N are all admitted supplied calendar members in the civil interval, including later sessions in the current civil period. Completed sessions are members with actual regular close<=C. They are independent of delivered prices, price-bearing volume, early-close scheduled nominal time and observed rows. Both require complete causal calendar proof; missing price never decreases either count. Early close compares the actual close, not scheduled_close_ns. Holidays absent from the admitted complete calendar create no expected market slot. Missing schedule proof makes both unavailable; it cannot establish a zero count.

For prior_complete require end<=T and all member sessions' actual closes<=C. Consume one DAILY row per member session, exactly its original session ID/start/end, including zero-volume sessions. Declare bars missing and reject any present target BAR role. Require all these DAILY slots, never skip a missing immediately preceding period. For current_to_cutoff consume only completed member sessions strictly before T from DAILY, plus a target BAR prefix when T belongs to membership and C>target regular open. Target DAILY is never consumed, including C>=target close; target contribution remains BAR to avoid double counting/route drift. Prefix end=min(C,actual regular close). No forming bar, later-session DAILY, future bar, bar straddling cutoff or interval outside its exact governed regular slot is silently filtered. Target at C<=regular open contributes no market observation and bars must be missing: previous completed member sessions still aggregate normally. An earlier session that should have completed by T but has close>C is a cutoff/config bounds error rather than silently omitted history.

DAILY rows must be chronological and exactly match required governed slots; absent row within an actual partial DAILY population is governed_gap. Absent entire required DAILY role is absent_input. An actual complete empty DAILY batch cannot certify a nonempty required session population. Empty required population needs no fake zero-width scope. Present BAR prefix has exact positive InputScope `[target.open,prefix_end)`, eligibility_policy `supplied_period`, matching explicit auction flags. Its canonical whole/interval coverage must certify the exact requested prefix; row count alone never proves temporal coverage. DAILY scope is positive span from first through last required regular slot with explicit per-session interval coverage and supplied_period policy; gaps between governed sessions are not fabricated observations. DAILY IntervalCoverage names are the original governed session IDs with exact regular bounds, expected1/observed0-or1 and truthful complete. BAR requires one IntervalCoverage named `period_prefix`, with exact InputScope bounds and its actual expected/observed/complete population; source coverage and per-interval declarations must not contradict. No DAILY role is permitted if its required slot population is empty. This proposed scope naming is a coordinator freeze choice below.

For the actual DAILY role, whole coverage.expected must equal D, coverage.observed equals delivered row_count, and every required slot has its explicit IntervalCoverage. Thus an empty partial delivery for D=1 is Coverage(1,0,false) and interval Coverage(1,0,false), giving incomplete_coverage/governed_gap with numerical quality expected1/observed0.
