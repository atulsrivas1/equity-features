## Closed proof population and causality

B means reversible canonical unpadded UTF8 base64url; full IDs<=128 codepoints/512 UTF8 bytes. Decode and exactly re-encode; malformed/overflow rejects. Classification history `h1:F:B(scope):B(P):B(cert)`, F=s/u/i; action history `a1:B(scope):B(P):B(cert)`; projection `r1:B(P):B(cert)`; source representation/quantity `q1:B(P):B(cert)`. P is actual target input_id, not an adapter alias. Histories bind their facts; source proofs bind market. Every pair matches target namespace, entity, source.source_id and source.snapshot_id; proof and target input_ids distinct. When target missing, supplied proofs must still coherently name one target/source/revision for that relationship; they cannot create its actual binding. Different action-facts and market source pairs need not share a publisher. Wrong target/source/revision is typed identity error, not failed/revision_mismatch; this precedence resolves the frozen proof_interval_rule against general diagnostic vocabulary.

History text historical/current_only/unknown; projection complete/partial/unknown. Source representation raw/caller_transformed; quantity raw_shares/split_shares. No price/factor payload in proofs or classification rows; factors have positive exact numerator/denominator, no text/price and no finite effective_end. Classifications require nonblank text when present; null/blank is a missing_text finding, not absence.

Every proof has a positive finite interval and original known_at<=K; classification proofs cover effective query, action history/projection cover whole requested scope, source representation/quantity proofs cover the actual market InputScope and all its selected observation times; history/projection cover the whole declared action scope. Completion is not inferred from snapshots or observed rows. Proof batches themselves must be complete with expected=observed=actual row count; an incomplete proof population cannot disambiguate or prove absence. A complete target projection additionally requires target Coverage.complete AND expected=observed=actual supplied row count BEFORE effective filtering. Empty complete0/0 differs from missing, unknown expected, and partial.

Intervals are half-open: start<=query<end; whole scope coverage start<=scope_start AND scope_end<=end. Membership effective_end may be null (unbounded), proof end may not. Count applicable memberships/proofs before known-at arbitration: >1 applicable row is DUPLICATE even if one is late/unknown or text agrees. Same-time canonical IDs give order only, never a winner. Canonical duplicate row identity/order errors precede assessment. A known future-effective membership is not active and cannot cause future_knowledge; an active required row/proof with known_at>K does. Named amendment EF1-REF-A1 supersedes contradictory original proof_interval_rule late-proof wording: structurally applicable required proof known_at>K is failed/future_knowledge; missing/expired proof is unknown. Required proof known_at=null is unknown/unknown_availability. Original known_at is required in known_at mode (including delayed evaluation) and reconstruction mode; do not call a mode helper that treats reconstruction as permission to use late/unknown facts. E never upgrades source knowledge, and future schedule knowledge is irrelevant here.

## Assessment and value semantics

Evaluate all independently visible conditions. Any visible failed comparison wins over unknown prerequisites; otherwise unknown prerequisites yield unknown; otherwise passed. Select scalar reason by the exact frozen lists in JSON. Failed order: action_policy_mismatch, revision_mismatch, wrong_scope, outside_effective_interval, future_knowledge, membership_absent. Unknown order: absent_reference, incomplete_reference, incomplete_projection, historical_proof_unknown, missing_text, unknown_availability. Passed reason none. Reasons are scalar strings, not additions to legacy Reason enum. Structural wrong source/target/revision remains ContractError. No proof applicability means unknown, not a known mismatch; outside_effective_interval requires an established required fact comparison, not an irrelevant row.

Classification: select exact requested entity/kind/scope query; one active nonblank causally admitted fact plus historical complete history/projection passes with actual text. Current_only/unknown history never proves historical membership, even if its effective interval and present text look plausible. Fully admitted complete population with no active fact gives failed/membership_absent, value null/not_applicable/no_eligible_observations. Missing population gives unknown/absent_reference. Missing proof or incomplete target/proof population never proves absence. Partial projection gives unknown/incomplete_projection and value null/incomplete_coverage/governed_gap. Future active row gives failed/future_knowledge and value null/missing_input/future_knowledge; unknown known-at gives unknown/unknown_availability and missing_input/unknown_availability. Nonblank membership text is preserved, not trimmed or translated.

Action gate: validate policy with existing ActionPolicy and exact ConfigSpec adjustment. raw/raw-v1 requires raw_shares/dividend none; split/split-factors-v1 uses dividend none; total_return/total-return-reinvest-v1 requires supplied_reinvestment_factors. apply_factors expects actual raw market adjustment, source representation raw and source quantity raw_shares. Requested output split_shares does not imply incoming raw volume is adjusted. caller_transformed expects market adjustment exactly policy adjustment, actual representation caller_transformed and actual quantity equal requested quantity. Known declaration mismatch produces failed/action_policy_mismatch; malformed policy is typed error. Actual action population/history/closed projection remain required even for raw; raw mode cannot silently infer complete no-action history. Raw ActionPolicy action_snapshot=none is a policy sentinel, not an actual source snapshot, and is not compared to coherent actual R1/R2. Only nonraw compares the requested action revision for revision_mismatch. Raw selects no transformation factors: every supplied factor row is structurally validated and retained in full input/population metadata, but its original known_at and factor value do not affect raw admission or witnesses because unused. Structural invalidity still rejects. Missing/incomplete actual action population remains unknown, never passed merely because raw. Causal market/history/projection/representation/quantity proofs remain required. Complete zero factor population with complete history/projection is no action, distinct from missing action facts.

Gate computes admission only. It never invokes apply_action_policy, mutates a batch, creates transformed prices, or adds cash dividends. Accepted utility mathematical application is a separate producer operation: for each observation t, multiply supplied price factors whose boundaries satisfy t<a<=anchor; split quantity divides by split factors only when requested split_shares; actual_notional multiplies both. Exact rational/integral representation and overflow rules remain; interval crossing an action boundary rejects. A caller_transformed input is not multiplied again. Reinvestment factors already encode dividends; no extra dividend addition. R03's 100/10/1000 ->50/20/1000 is an independent utility expectation, never a gate output.
