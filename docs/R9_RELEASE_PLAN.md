Hash consistency does not authenticate supplied facts or prove source rights, original execution, mathematical correctness, historical acquisition time, economic truth or regulatory compliance.

R3's accepted contracts/results/registry/compatibility/public examples supply the minimum dependency. R4/R5/R6 provenance and R8 producer/scanner/label identities are reusable accepted context, not authority to acquire actual data. 1. **Closed supported content and canonical bytes:** list the exact accepted typed batches/result graphs, schema/version domains and trusted replay feature IDs. Specify signed-int/UTCns/float64 signed-zero/nonfinite/Decimal/null/bool/unit semantics, stable order and prohibited ambiguities. 2. **Receipt truth and availability:** bind C/K/E cutoffs separately from a supplied execution timestamp; no hidden wall-clock read in pure code. Specify unknown proof/omission/truncation/caller assertions and schema compatibility. Distinguish structural validity, content match, replay match and independent mathematical validation rather than reducing everything to one PASS flag.
3. **Safe transport and finite controls:** freeze separate limits for manifest bytes, member count, compressed/decompressed bytes, record/array/string/depth/node counts and replay jobs/time/cancellation under the supported caller/platform. Validate aggregate sizes before materialization. Finite application controls do not imply whole-kernel physical enforcement; unknown completion remains unknown. Parser failures must not hide cleanup outcomes.

No automatic dependency download or arbitrary execution from a bundle.
