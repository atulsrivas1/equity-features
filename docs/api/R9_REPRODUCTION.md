## Retention, rights and threat model

APIs persist no files/cache and impose no library TTL. Caller owns returned bytes/objects, access controls, permitted retention and cleanup. Experimental CI artifacts expire after30days; reviewed source/current artifacts are the channel, not stable registry or permanent availability. Retain permitted input/config/result/archive bytes before expiry if future reproduction is needed. Digests cannot recover missing content.

Fixtures are caller-created synthetic data. Rights/environment/publication fields remain declarations, not authentication. CRC/hash consistency detects inconsistent bytes; malicious producers can provide self-consistent false facts. Bounded data-only parsing and fixed installed replay are not arbitrary-code sandboxing. Qualification creates new owned directories with ordinary cleanup, not bounded physical cleanup. No provider, profitability or regulatory compliance certification.
