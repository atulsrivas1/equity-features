# Portable origin-context quote selector clarification 1

Status: narrow proposed contract clarification for fresh independent joint mathematics/boundary review; no implementation, source qualification, freeze or publication authority. This note supplements the sealed boundary draft3, mathematics amendment draft3 and their separately pinned clarifications without changing their bytes, schemas, codec inventory, error codes or formula branches. It defines the portable origin-context selection that the earlier phrase "original causal quote" did not fully specify. R25 and original44 contracts are unchanged.

## 1. Origin view and candidate context controls

For each admitted execution root let t be its immutable new execution event, A its immutable signing_A_ns, and R the original new execution revision. Current price/quantity remain the selected K revision. The signing view uses only records known_at<=A and admitted by the A cursor frontier (positions known<=A and within the selected K cursors). Chain reconstruction uses actual stream positions and target_revision_id edges. No stream name ordering is a causal relationship; there is no same-stream requirement or quote-before-execution-position requirement across streams. Later K-visible/A-hidden revisions cannot repair, cancel, replace or age original context.

The operation's context_ids are an explicit K-admitted candidate pool. Missing named identity is stage5 missing_identity at its context_ids entry; a named K/cursor-hidden control is stage7 temporal_cut at that entry, even when another control could suffice. For each root, first exclude A-hidden candidates using their actual stamp/cursor only; their payload cannot establish coverage, membership, absence or availability. Among A-visible context_prefix controls with matching scope/contract/session/clock/bases, complete=true, decision=not_applicable, reason=captured, a sufficient full quote prefix/domain and coherent expected membership, select the earliest by (known_at_ns,stream_id ASCII,position,control_id ASCII) ascending. This tie order only selects a certificate; it does not order source events. The control's own stamp.event>=event_domain.end, stamp/cursors, expected_contract_ids and expected_root_ids must satisfy the existing control invariants. The required finite search domain is [Scope.partition_start_ns,t], both endpoints inclusive; eligible quote roots must lie in it. A covering control may have a larger domain (for example control[0,12] can cover scope start1 through execution10). A domain truncated to the winning quote cannot prove latest selection. No pre-partition quote fallback is admitted. expected_root_ids here are immutable quote_id values, with each original and relevant A revision chain retained. The control's expected membership includes cancelled and condition-excluded roots; its exclusion annotation does not turn an active excluded quote into a removable candidate.

No sufficient A-visible complete prefix gives side=unknown, reason=incomplete_context, null quote_revision_id and null pairing_id. It does not give certified absence or authorize searching partial history for a usable fallback. A valid incomplete certificate is typed incompleteness; a contradictory complete certificate is stage7 identity_conflict at its context_ids pointer. Merely unselected pool controls do not enter selected dependencies or context hash. Selected K capture is independent of this A-context requirement.

## 2. Epsilon latest-active selector

Within that certified session prefix, match exact execution scope_id and contract_id, all underlying/session/currency/action/deliverable/definition basis/clock dimensions, and immutable quote event<=t. Reconstruct each matching quote root at A. Cancelled roots are removed. Rank remaining ACTIVE roots by (immutable quote event_ns, immutable quote_id ASCII) and select the maximum. Replacements preserve that immutable rank; revision ID, stream ID, receipt position, bid/ask quality and age are not ranking keys. There is no future-event quote, cross-session search, nearest-time heuristic or fallback to an earlier good market.

No active matching root gives unknown/missing_quote with null quote_revision_id. Otherwise quote_revision_id names the exact selected active A revision, including when stale, condition-excluded, one-sided, nonpositive or crossed. Compute age=t-quote.event_ns as an exact nonnegative integer. age>max_quote_age_ns gives unknown/stale_quote; equality is admitted. Then condition excluded, missing side, incompatible signing representation/scale or failed market inequalities give unknown/invalid_quote. Selected incompatible declared operation units/bases remain the existing stage6 identity_mismatch where required by the base schema; the market-quality branch never coerces representations or suppresses an admission error. Nonfinite operand bits remain stage4 invalid_scalar. For a valid B64 epsilon market p>0,b>0,h>=b, apply the sealed ask-first RN formulas exactly: classified buy/sell, or unknown/dead_band. A locked valid epsilon quote is not automatically invalid. pairing_id is null for epsilon.

Known cancellation witnesses needed to prove this maximum are exactly the matching cancelled roots whose immutable (event,quote_id) rank exceeds the selected active rank. If no active root exists, retain every matching cancelled root in the certified prefix. Lower-ranked cancelled roots, wrong-scope/contract/basis roots and event>t roots are unselected. An ACTIVE condition-excluded/invalid/stale maximum stays selected and unknown; it is never treated as cancellation permitting fallback.

## 3. Exact paired origin selector

Use the immutable original execution's exact paired_quote_revision_id and its exact Pairing. Pairing.root_id and original_revision_id equal this root and R; its quote_revision_id equals that immutable pointer; the named Quote.pairing_id agrees. Retained dangling references or contradictory links remain stage5 missing_identity/identity_conflict or stage6 identity_mismatch at the actual offending field under existing precedence. Do not reinterpret a malformed graph as ordinary missingness.

The exact pairing, quote and identity ancestors must be A/cursor-visible, same scope/contract/clock/bases, inside the admitted [Scope.partition_start_ns,t] session prefix and effective-compatible with the original definition context. The paired quote revision must equal its quote root's ACTIVE A revision. Missing original pairing/pointer, no A-admissible pairing, an exact paired revision superseded/cancelled by A, or its actual visible quote event outside that prefix gives unknown/missing_pairing; never substitute the newer quote or any other root. For supersession/cancellation retain the exact paired revision plus the actual A replacing/cancelling chain that proves this disposition; an out-of-prefix exact quote retains its actual fact provenance without becoming an admitted signing quote. K-only later changes are ignored. A retained A-hidden candidate is not hashed/read as if selected; no A-visible proof means incompleteness/missing pairing, not a future-value proof.

With complete context and an admitted exact active pair, retain its quote_revision_id and pairing_id. Apply the same exact event-relative inclusive age test, then paired market validity (positive exact compatible integer p,b,h with b<h). Stale precedes invalid; stale=>unknown/stale_quote, invalid=>unknown/invalid_quote. Valid p<=b is sell/classified, p>=h is buy/classified, strictly inside is unknown/inside_spread. No epsilon, tick, nearest-quote or earlier-good fallback applies. For missing_pairing retain pairing_id only when an actual A-admissible Pairing was selected and quote_revision_id only when its actual A-admissible exact quote was retained; otherwise each is null independently. Incomplete_context takes precedence over selecting any pair or quote.

## 4. Exact selected context set, hashing and availability

Keep the sealed context hash domain and encoding:

`SHA256(UTF8('option-flow-variants-context1\n') || J([root_id,A,policy_record_hash,ordered_selected_context_record_hashes]))`.

The selected stamped context record set is exactly: R; the chosen context_prefix Control if any; the actual original DefinitionUse Definition or absence/ineligibility Control (and visible ineligible Definition when required); the selected quote root's new..selected-A revision chain; the necessary cancellation chains from section2; and, for paired signing, the actual selected Pairing, exact paired quote and new..active-A replacing/cancelling chain necessary under section3. Include chain ancestors only as needed to resolve that selected identity and disposition, through the A terminal/active revision; deduplicate by (record_type,record_id). A quote uses revision_id as record_id; execution uses revision_id, Definition uses definition_id, Control uses control_id, Pairing uses pairing_id. When incomplete context prevents quote/pair selection, only the actual original execution and actual selected DefinitionUse records are in this set. An unavailable DefinitionUse never reads or hashes a future Definition.

Hash each complete selected named record with its registered record_digest. Sort entries by (stamp.stream_id ASCII,stamp.position,record_type ASCII,record_id ASCII), then take those record hashes in that order. Every member individually passes A and applicable cursors. Include no whole Reference, CivilFact/SpotFact unused by signing, unselected controls, other quote history, K economic correction, whole Snapshot, Binding descriptor or Scope descriptor in this list. Policy is bound once by policy_record_hash outside the list. Binding provenance descriptors and Scope/Policy remain their exact atemporal Evidence dependencies; they cannot prove quote coverage. The current K execution/economics is separately bound by Result and economic dependencies. Full Snapshot/Request provenance independently binds the candidate packet, not the origin-context maximum.

Every selected stamped record contributes a context Dependency with its existing registered record type/identity/hash and actual known_at; needed removal chains are Quote/use=context, not a new dependency type. DefinitionUse and atemporal dependencies retain their already prescribed uses. Evidence.control_ids includes the actually selected prefix and actually used absence/ineligibility controls, not every candidate. Include binding dependencies for selected records under the existing Binding rules. available_at is the maximum of actual selected dependency clocks and other actual operation economic/capture prerequisites. A quote chain's necessary ancestors do not import unrelated history or K-only clocks. No current signing time, hash acquisition time, nominal A or K is invented as a dependency clock. Context completeness attests supplied coherent coverage, not external truth; worker qualification remains separately required.

## 5. Required independent literal distinctions and disposition

Independent full-record/hash/error fixtures must distinguish: two same-event active roots (ASCII quote_id maximum), replacements retaining root rank, cross-stream position ordering having no priority, quote.event>t rejection despite known_at<=A, age exactly max and max+1 using t rather than A, latest invalid/excluded with earlier good quote, latest cancelled permitting next active plus minimal removal set, K-only cancellation preserving A sign, no active roots with all necessary cancellation evidence, A-hidden candidate prefixes, earliest sufficient prefix ties, incomplete prefix versus certified missing quote, paired supersession/cancellation at A versus K-only later change, malformed links versus typed missing pairing, locked epsilon versus locked paired, stale+invalid precedence, and adding an unselected future Reference component/control without changing context hash or availability. No numerical expectations may be generated by the production evaluator/serializer.

These are explicit portable selector semantics. Historical source callers and original44 do not establish their cross-stream/tie/cancellation behavior by implication. No source caller, formatter/compiler, captured-dataset or adapter qualification is claimed.
