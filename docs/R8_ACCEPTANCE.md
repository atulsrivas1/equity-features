## Delivered design and qualification boundaries

The core contracts/features pair remains version0.0.4a4 and source independent. Optional strategies and labels distributions are version0.1.0a1, depend only on contracts, and keep acquisition, persistence and publication outside calculations. Scanner results bind complete producer facts, stable ranking, readiness and explicit missing-universe coverage. Forward labels bind immutable signal origins, complete supplied price paths and maturity/known-at/revision/basis identity. The consumer joins explicitly by as-of time. Supplied adjusted paths are facts; these packages do not calculate corporate actions or infer trading profitability.

The chronological example executes the actual core producer, scanner and labels, checks independent selections/coverage/returns and rejections, and preserves complete signal bytes under later future-label facts. Installed qualification uses fresh Windows/Linux CPython3.12 wheel and sdist component environments plus separate combined consumer forms. Core-only imports and optional-package absence are checked before research installation. Archive source/RECORD/sdist maps, installed origins, typing, causal fixtures and complete core parity are required; package isolation is observed in these environments, not an all-platform claim.

Delivery uses reviewed GitHub source and finite-retention CI artifacts. It does not assert PyPI/stable publication, indefinite artifact availability, alpha, fills/costs/portfolio behavior, provider rights or production capacity beyond the measured synthetic population. Native, worker, spill and output-layout conclusions must follow the actual EQ088 evidence and retain failed/inconclusive results; no production default is changed by a benchmark proposal.
